# Meet Folsk

We're building a completely free dating alternative for adults, centered on mutual interest, conversation, and playful expression.

Folsk's product principles are simple: likes, matches, messages, discovery preferences, and safety tools should be free. No subscriptions, paid boosts, or paid dating advantages. Optional gifts are earned through participation and shared only between mutual matches; they never create an obligation to respond.

## Built around mutual choice

- Messaging and gifts require an active mutual match.
- Points and gifts never determine discovery rank or access to people.
- Privacy, blocking, reporting, and account controls are part of the core product.
- No public popularity rankings or punitive streaks.

## In development

Folsk is under active development. These repositories contain implementation work, selected designs, and draft contracts; they do not represent a launched public dating service. Age assurance, moderation operations, and launch readiness remain work in progress.

Explore the repositories for implementation details, and open an issue in the relevant project to report a bug or discuss an improvement.
